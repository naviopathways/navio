Three ways to stand behind the work

Choose the level that fits your organization.

Each package adds more visibility and more ways to connect with Navio NEXT participants. Reach out and we’ll walk you through the details.

Visibility

Silver

$199

For organizations that want to back Navio NEXT and be seen by participants throughout the conference.

1 rep attends

  • Logo on Navio NEXT’s introduction slides
  • Logo on promotional materials
  • Recognition on Navio Pathways’ Instagram and website
  • Opportunity to provide brochures or promotional material to participants
  • Certificate of appreciation at the end of the conference

Visibility + interaction

Gold

$399

For organizations that want more exposure and the chance to talk with participants about their work and careers.

Up to 2 reps

  • Additional logo placements on conference promotional materials
  • Space for a company-provided banner with your logo, slogan, and contact information
  • Recognition on social media and the website
  • Recognition in the closing speech and introduction slideshow
  • Opportunity to distribute branded merchandise to participants
  • Opportunity to network with students and speak about your organization and careers within it

Maximum contribution

Diamond

$499

For organizations that want the most impact: significant visibility and direct engagement with participants and attendees.

Up to 4 reps

  • Featured logo placement throughout conference materials
  • Recognition in the opening and closing speeches
  • Dedicated sponsor feature on Navio Pathways’ social media and website
  • Dedicated booth or tent for networking
  • Approved interactive activities or workshops led by your representatives
  • Designated networking session for participants, led by your representatives
  • Featured recognition as a Diamond sponsor

Benefits include: Logo/name recognition in promotional materials, verbal recognition during opening speech, Certificate of appreciation
