Youth safety

Safety needs structure, not assumptions.

This public summary sets expectations for communication and youth-focused programs, including in-person events.

✦

Core expectations

  • Treat every young person with dignity and respect.
  • Keep program communication transparent and use approved channels.
  • Do not request unnecessary personal information or private contact.
  • Use clear supervision, consent, privacy, and reporting procedures.
  • Act on concerns and prohibit retaliation for good-faith reports.

Program communication

Program and event communication, adult involvement, venue access, and supervision must follow the procedures issued for the relevant activity. Adults contributing to youth-facing activity are expected to maintain professional boundaries.

Report a concern

Email hello@naviopathways.com with “Youth safety concern” in the subject line. If someone is in immediate danger, contact local emergency services.
